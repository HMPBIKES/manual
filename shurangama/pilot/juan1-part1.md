# 《楞严经》英译小样 · 卷一（一）

**范围：** 序分 → 阿难请定 → 如来许说 → 征心所在 → 七处破妄第一处（破执心在身内）

**底本：** 法界佛教总会《大佛顶首楞严经》简体经文，已与 CBETA 大正藏 T945 初步对照。法总本有一处段落移位（见第 10 段）。

**义理依据：** 宣化上人《楞严经浅释》第二册（以下简称《浅释》）。与其他注疏读法不同的地方在注释里标出。

**分段：** 依《浅释》附录《简要科判》。

**每段四栏：** 原文、解析（中文，供审核我的理解）、Translation、Notes。

## 1　证信序·列众（声闻众）

**原文**

> 如是我闻，一时佛在室罗筏城，祇桓精舍。与大比丘众，千二百五十人俱。皆是无漏大阿罗汉，佛子住持，善超诸有；能于国土，成就威仪；从佛转轮，妙堪遗嘱；严净毗尼，弘范三界；应身无量，度脱众生；拔济未来，越诸尘累。

**解析**

- 前两句具六成就：如是（信）、我闻（闻）、一时（时）、佛（主）、室罗筏城祇桓精舍（处）、大比丘众（众）。
- 室罗筏城即舍卫城（Śrāvastī）；祇桓精舍即祇树给孤独园（Jetavana）。
- **佛子住持：** 《浅释》说"佛子"指在座这些大阿罗汉，不是罗睺罗。他们"从佛口生，从法化生"，所以称佛子。"住持"是住于佛法、持守坚固之定，使佛法延续不断。
- **善超诸有：** 超出三界二十五有。
- **能于国土，成就威仪：** 能往十方国土，一举一动有威可畏、有仪可象。
- **从佛转轮：** 随佛助转法轮，不是指轮回。
- **妙堪遗嘱：** 有不可思议的智慧，堪受佛的付嘱，荷担如来家业。
- **严净毗尼，弘范三界：** 持戒严谨清净，作三界的模范。
- **应身无量，度脱众生：** 现无量应化身，度众生得解脱。
- **拔济未来，越诸尘累：** 救拔未来众生（《浅释》说"我们都在这'未来'数目里头"），令其超越尘劳的系累。

**Translation**

> Thus have I heard. At one time the Buddha was at the Jetavana Monastery in the city of Śrāvastī, together with a great assembly of twelve hundred fifty bhikṣus. All were great arhats free of outflows—true sons of the Buddha who upheld the Dharma and had passed beyond every form of existence. In every land they went to, their deportment inspired reverence. They followed the Buddha in turning the Dharma wheel and were fully worthy to be entrusted with his legacy. Strict and pure in the Vinaya, they were models for the three realms. Manifesting countless response-bodies, they ferried beings to liberation, and they would rescue those of the future, carrying them beyond the entanglements of the dust.

**Notes**

1. *Free of outflows* (無漏, *anāsrava*): no longer leaking attention and energy outward through the senses into afflicted states; synonymous with the arhat's attainment.
2. *Sons of the Buddha* (佛子): per the Venerable Master Hsuan Hua's commentary, the term refers to the arhats themselves, "born from the Buddha's mouth, transformed from the Dharma," not to Rāhula, the Buddha's son by birth.
3. *Every form of existence* (諸有): the twenty-five modes of existence (二十五有) that make up the three realms.
4. *Those of the future* (未來): the commentary stresses that present-day readers are among "those of the future" whom the arhats rescue.

## 2　证信序·列众（缘觉众、初心众）

**原文**

> 其名曰：大智舍利弗、摩诃目犍连、摩诃拘絺罗、富楼那弥多罗尼子、须菩提、优婆尼沙陀等，而为上首。复有无量辟支无学，并其初心，同来佛所，属诸比丘，休夏自恣。

**解析**

- 上首六位依次是：舍利弗（智慧第一）、目犍连、摩诃拘絺罗、富楼那、须菩提、优婆尼沙陀（后文二十五圆通中以色尘圆通者）。
- **辟支无学：** 《浅释》把它读作"已到无学位的辟支佛"，即一个整体，不是"辟支"与"无学"两类人。
- **并其初心：** 连同初发心、道业尚未成熟的声闻和缘觉。
- **属诸比丘：** 《浅释》把"属"读作随附，即他们随附诸大比丘。CBETA 的标点是"……同来佛所。属诸比丘休夏自恣，十方菩萨咨决心疑"，把"属"读作"适值、正当"：正值比丘们夏安居结束行自恣，十方菩萨前来请决心疑。英译依《浅释》，注释并列两说。
- **异文：** "优婆尼沙陀"大正藏作"优波尼沙陀"。
- **休夏自恣：** 夏安居（四月十五至七月十五）圆满之日行自恣，请大众举其过失。

**Translation**

> Foremost among them were Śāriputra of great wisdom, Mahāmaudgalyāyana, Mahākauṣṭhila, Pūrṇamaitrāyaṇīputra, Subhūti, Upaniṣad, and others. There were also countless pratyekabuddhas beyond training, together with practitioners who had only just set out on the path. They had all come to where the Buddha was and joined the bhikṣus at the conclusion of the summer retreat for the Pravāraṇā ceremony.

**Notes**

1. *Pratyekabuddhas beyond training* (辟支無學): the commentary reads this as one group—pratyekabuddhas who have reached the stage of "no more learning" (*aśaikṣa*). Some commentators read two groups: pratyekabuddhas, and śrāvakas beyond training.
2. *Joined the bhikṣus* (屬諸比丘): the commentary reads 屬 as "attached to, joining." The CBETA punctuation instead reads 屬 as "it happened to be": "It was the time of the bhikṣus' Pravāraṇā at the end of the summer retreat, and bodhisattvas from the ten directions had come…"
3. *Pravāraṇā* (自恣): the ceremony closing the three-month rains retreat, at which each monastic invites the assembly to point out any faults seen, heard, or suspected during the retreat.

## 3　证信序·列众（菩萨众）

**原文**

> 十方菩萨，咨决心疑，钦奉慈严，将求密义。即时如来，敷座宴安，为诸会中，宣示深奥，法筵清众，得未曾有。迦陵仙音，遍十方界。恒沙菩萨，来聚道场，文殊师利，而为上首。

**解析**

- **咨决心疑：** 请佛决断心中未明之处。
- **钦奉慈严：** "慈严"指佛，慈而且严。
- **将求密义：** 所求的就是本经的"密因"了义。《浅释》说连菩萨都不明白这个道理，所以来请问。
- **敷座宴安：** 佛敷座安然而坐。
- **迦陵仙音：** 迦陵频伽（kalaviṅka），意为妙声鸟，比喻佛音婉转美妙，并非指鸟声本身。
- **文殊师利：** 菩萨中智慧第一，为菩萨众上首。

**Translation**

> Bodhisattvas from the ten directions had come to seek resolution of the doubts in their minds. Revering the One both compassionate and stern, they hoped to receive the secret meaning. Then the Tathāgata arranged his seat and sat at ease, and for the whole gathering he proclaimed the profound and recondite. The pure assembly at that Dharma banquet gained what it had never had before. His voice, like the call of the kalaviṅka bird, pervaded the realms of the ten directions, and bodhisattvas as numerous as the Ganges' sands gathered at the place of practice, with Mañjuśrī foremost among them.

**Notes**

1. *The secret meaning* (密義): the "secret cause" (密因) named in the sutra's full title—the hidden basis of the Tathāgatas' realization, which the sutra's first four fascicles set out to reveal.
2. *Kalaviṅka*: a Himalayan bird proverbial for its exquisite song, said to be audible even from within the egg; a standard image for the Buddha's voice.

## 4　发起序·斋会分领，阿难独归

**原文**

> 时波斯匿王，为其父王讳日营斋，请佛宫掖，自迎如来，广设珍馐，无上妙味，兼复亲延诸大菩萨。城中复有长者居士，同时饭僧，伫佛来应。佛敕文殊，分领菩萨及阿罗汉，应诸斋主。唯有阿难，先受别请，远游未还，不遑僧次。

**解析**

- **讳日营斋：** 讳日即父王忌日。《浅释》说这一天恰是七月十五，即自恣日，与上段"休夏自恣"相应。
- **宫掖：** 宫中、内廷。
- **伫佛来应：** 等候佛来应供。
- **佛敕文殊，分领……应诸斋主：** 佛亲自到王宫应供，文殊分领其余菩萨和阿罗汉，到各位长者居士家应供。
- **唯有阿难，先受别请：** 阿难事先受了另外的邀请，远行未回。
- **不遑僧次：** 来不及排入僧众的应供次序。

**Translation**

> At that time King Prasenajit, holding a meal offering on the anniversary of his father's death, invited the Buddha to the inner palace. He welcomed the Tathāgata in person and set out a lavish array of the rarest delicacies, and he personally invited the great bodhisattvas as well. Elders and lay devotees throughout the city were likewise offering meals to the Saṅgha and waiting for the Buddha to come. The Buddha instructed Mañjuśrī to divide the bodhisattvas and arhats among the various hosts. Only Ānanda, having earlier accepted a separate invitation, had traveled far and not yet returned, and so he missed his place in the Saṅgha's order.

**Notes**

1. *Anniversary of his father's death* (諱日): the commentary notes that this day coincided with the fifteenth of the seventh lunar month—the very day of the Pravāraṇā mentioned above—traditionally a day of offerings on behalf of deceased parents.
2. *His place in the Saṅgha's order* (僧次): invitations to the Saṅgha were filled by turn in order of seniority; Ānanda, being away, was left out of the rotation.

## 5　发起序·阿难独行乞食

**原文**

> 既无上座，及阿阇黎，途中独归。其日无供，即时阿难，执持应器，于所游城，次第循乞。心中初求，最后檀越，以为斋主，无问净秽，刹利尊姓，及旃陀罗。

**解析**

- **上座、阿阇黎：** 出家人出行，按规矩应有上座（受戒二十年以上的长老）或阿阇黎（轨范师）同行。阿难此时两者都没有，独自一人回来。这一点是后面出事的伏笔。
- **应器：** 钵（pātra），"应量器"的略称。
- **次第循乞：** 按门户顺序挨家乞食，不加拣择。
- **心中初求，最后檀越，以为斋主：** 《浅释》的读法是：阿难起初就打定主意，乞到最后，谁施给他足够的饮食，谁就是他的斋主。另一种常见读法是：阿难有意以"最后"、还未曾供过僧的施主为斋主。英译依《浅释》，注释并列两说。
- **无问净秽，刹利尊姓，及旃陀罗：** 不问洁净与否，从刹帝利贵族到旃陀罗贱民，一律平等乞食。

**Translation**

> Having neither an elder nor an ācārya with him, he was returning alone. There was no meal provided for him that day, so Ānanda took up his alms bowl and went begging from door to door, in order, through the city he was passing. At the outset he resolved in his mind that whoever was the last to give would be his patron for the day—regardless of whether the household was pure or impure, whether of noble kṣatriya lineage or caṇḍāla.

**Notes**

1. *Elder* (上座) and *ācārya* (阿闍黎): monastic rules called for a junior monk traveling outside to be accompanied by a senior elder or a preceptor. Ānanda's being alone sets the stage for what follows.
2. *Whoever was the last to give* (最後檀越): following the commentary, Ānanda would let his round end wherever it ended, taking whoever completed his meal as his patron. Another traditional reading takes 最後 as "the last remaining donor"—someone who had not yet had the chance to make an offering to the Saṅgha.
3. *Caṇḍāla* (旃陀羅): the lowest, "untouchable" stratum of ancient Indian society, associated with butchery and other occupations deemed defiling.

## 6　发起序·阿难行平等慈

**原文**

> 方行等慈，不择微贱，发意圆成，一切众生无量功德。阿难已知，如来世尊，诃须菩提及大迦叶，为阿罗汉，心不均平。钦仰如来，开阐无遮，度诸疑谤。经彼城隍，徐步郭门，严整威仪，肃恭斋法。

**解析**

- **方行等慈：** 正要实践平等的慈心。
- **发意圆成，一切众生无量功德：** 心意是要成就一切众生供养福田的无量功德。
- **诃须菩提及大迦叶：** 须菩提专向富人乞食，大迦叶专向穷人乞食。二人都有拣择，所以佛呵斥他们身为阿罗汉而心不平等。
- **开阐无遮：** 佛的教法开放、平等，没有遮限。
- **度诸疑谤：** 化解外人对僧团偏颇的怀疑和诽谤。
- **城隍：** 城壕，有水为池，无水为隍。
- **郭门：** 外城门。

**Translation**

> He meant to practice impartial kindness, not passing over the lowly, intending to bring to fulfillment the boundless merit of all beings. Ānanda knew that the Tathāgata, the World-Honored One, had rebuked Subhūti and Mahākāśyapa because, though arhats, their minds were not impartial. He revered the Tathāgata's teaching, which is open to all without restriction and frees people from doubt and slander. Crossing the city moat, he walked slowly through the outer gate, his deportment strict and orderly, solemnly observing the rules for receiving alms.

**Notes**

1. *Rebuked Subhūti and Mahākāśyapa*: Subhūti preferred to beg from the wealthy, so that the poor need not spend their scarce means; Mahākāśyapa preferred to beg from the poor, so that they might plant blessings for the future. Both, the Buddha said, were discriminating.
2. *Boundless merit of all beings*: by accepting alms from anyone at all, Ānanda gives every household an equal chance to plant blessings by making an offering.

## 7　正堕之事·摩登伽女咒摄

**原文**

> 尔时阿难，因乞食次，经历淫室，遭大幻术，摩登伽女，以娑毗迦罗先梵天咒，摄入淫席，淫躬抚摩，将毁戒体。

**解析**

- **淫室：** 摩登伽女所住的娼家。
- **娑毗迦罗：** 《浅释》译为"黄发外道"。摩登伽女的咒术是这位外道传下来的。
- **先梵天咒：** 外道假托为古时梵天所传的咒，以取信于人。
- **摄入淫席：** 阿难被咒迷住神识，不由自主地被摄到淫席上。
- **将毁戒体：** 关键在"将"字。《浅释》一再强调，是"将毁而还没有毁"，阿难并未破戒。英译必须保留这一层。

**Translation**

> Then, in the course of his alms round, Ānanda passed by a house of prostitution and fell under a powerful spell of illusion. The Mātaṅga woman, using a spell of the Kapila sect that was claimed to come from the Brahma heavens of old, drew him onto her bed. She caressed him with her lascivious body, and he was on the verge of breaking his precepts.

**Notes**

1. *On the verge of* (將): the commentary insists that Ānanda's precepts were about to be broken but were not; the Buddha's intervention came before any transgression.
2. *Kapila sect* (娑毗迦羅): the commentary glosses the name as "the yellow-haired non-Buddhist teachers," from whom the woman had learned her spell.
3. *Claimed to come from the Brahma heavens of old* (先梵天咒): the attribution to the god Brahmā is a pretense used to win credence; the spell's real source was the Kapila teachers.
4. *Precepts* (戒體): literally, "the precept-substance"—the inner disposition conferred at ordination that sustains a monastic's vows.

## 8　救堕之事·化佛宣咒，文殊往护

**原文**

> 如来知彼，淫术所加，斋毕旋归。王及大臣，长者居士，俱来随佛，愿闻法要。于时世尊，顶放百宝无畏光明，光中出生千叶宝莲，有佛化身，结跏趺坐，宣说神咒。敕文殊师利，将咒往护。恶咒销灭，提奖阿难及摩登伽，归来佛所。

**解析**

- **斋毕旋归：** 佛知道阿难被淫术所加，应供一完毕就回到精舍。
- **百宝无畏光明：** 《浅释》说"百宝"可表百界，"千叶宝莲"可表千如。无畏光明能降伏一切天魔外道。
- **有佛化身……宣说神咒：** 化佛所说的就是〈楞严咒〉。《浅释》特别指出，这是诸咒中唯一由化佛宣说的。
- **将咒往护：** 文殊持咒前往护持阿难。
- **提奖阿难及摩登伽：** "提"是扶持刚从咒中醒来、神志未清的阿难；"奖"是以好言劝勉摩登伽女。

**Translation**

> The Tathāgata knew that Ānanda had fallen prey to her lewd arts, and as soon as the meal was over he returned. The king, his ministers, the elders, and the lay devotees all came along with the Buddha, wishing to hear the essentials of the Dharma. Then from the crown of his head the World-Honored One emitted a fearless light of a hundred jewels. Within the light appeared a thousand-petaled jeweled lotus, upon which sat a transformation body of the Buddha in full lotus posture, proclaiming a spiritual mantra. The Buddha instructed Mañjuśrī to take the mantra and go to protect Ānanda. The evil spell was dissolved, and Mañjuśrī, supporting Ānanda and encouraging the Mātaṅga woman, brought them both back to where the Buddha was.

**Notes**

1. *Spiritual mantra* (神咒): the Śūraṅgama Mantra, given in full in Fascicle Seven. The commentary notes that it is the only mantra spoken by a transformation Buddha emerging from the Buddha's crown, marking it as "the king of mantras."
2. *A hundred jewels … a thousand petals*: the commentary suggests these may symbolize the hundred dharma-realms and the thousand suchnesses (百界千如) of Tiantai doctrine.
3. *Supporting … encouraging* (提獎): 提 is helping Ānanda, still dazed from the spell, to his feet; 獎 is speaking kind words to the Mātaṅga woman so that she would come willingly.

## 9　阿难请定

**原文**

> 阿难见佛，顶礼悲泣，恨无始来，一向多闻，未全道力。殷勤启请，十方如来得成菩提，妙奢摩他、三摩、禅那，最初方便。于时复有恒沙菩萨，及诸十方大阿罗汉、辟支佛等，俱愿乐闻，退坐默然，承受圣旨。

**解析**

- **恨：** 《浅释》说这个"恨"字是阿难觉悟的表现，就是悔改。
- **一向多闻，未全道力：** 生生世世偏重多闻，定力没有修成。《浅释》同时提醒，不能因此走向另一个偏端，只修定不学教。
- **殷勤启请，十方如来得成菩提：** 《浅释》特别强调，阿难是向释迦牟尼佛请问"十方如来是怎样成就菩提的"，而不是"启请十方如来"。英译的句法必须体现这一点。
- **妙奢摩他、三摩、禅那：** 奢摩他（止）、三摩（三摩钵提，观）、禅那（止观双运的静虑）。《浅释》指出，"妙"字是阿难加的，佛平时不这样说。
- **最初方便：** 入手的方便法门。

**Translation**

> When Ānanda saw the Buddha, he bowed his head to the ground and wept, filled with remorse that from beginningless time he had devoted himself to learning and had not perfected the strength of the Way. He earnestly asked the Buddha how the Tathāgatas of the ten directions had attained bodhi—what the initial expedient was for the wondrous śamatha, samāpatti, and dhyāna. At that time bodhisattvas as numerous as the Ganges' sands, together with great arhats, pratyekabuddhas, and others from the ten directions, all longed to hear the answer. They withdrew to their seats in silence to receive the sage's teaching.

**Notes**

1. *Asked the Buddha how the Tathāgatas … had attained bodhi* (啟請十方如來得成菩提): the commentary warns against reading this as Ānanda petitioning the Buddhas of the ten directions. He is asking Śākyamuni to explain how they attained bodhi.
2. *Śamatha, samāpatti, dhyāna* (奢摩他、三摩、禪那): stilling, contemplation, and their union in meditative absorption—the three aspects of practice around which the sutra's teaching is structured. In this sutra 三摩 abbreviates 三摩鉢提 (*samāpatti*).
3. *Wondrous* (妙): the commentary notes that Ānanda adds this word himself; the Buddha, hearing it, knows that Ānanda does not yet understand the samādhi by which Buddhas are made.

## 10　如来许说，示定总名

**原文**

> 尔时世尊在大众中，舒金色臂，摩阿难顶，告示阿难，及诸大众：有三摩提，名大佛顶首楞严王，具足万行；十方如来，一门超出妙庄严路。汝今谛听！阿难顶礼，伏受慈旨。

**解析**

- **舒金色臂，摩阿难顶：** 安慰、摄受阿难。
- **三摩提：** 即定、三昧。
- **大佛顶首楞严王：** 定的名称。《浅释》释为"究竟坚固"之定，是诸定之王。
- **具足万行：** 万行和一切三昧都含摄在内。
- **一门超出妙庄严路：** 十方诸佛都是从这一门超出生死、成就佛果，这是一条微妙庄严的道路。
- **位置：** 大正藏 T945 中，这一段原本夹在第 15 段讲堂问答的中间，在"故我在堂，得远瞻见"之后、"佛告阿难：如汝所言"之前。法总经文依圆瑛法师的意见把它移到这里，《浅释》也照此讲解。

**Translation**

> Then the World-Honored One, in the midst of the great assembly, extended his golden arm, rubbed the crown of Ānanda's head, and declared to Ānanda and the whole assembly: "There is a samādhi called the Great Buddha's Crown Śūraṅgama King, complete with the myriad practices. It is the single gateway through which the Tathāgatas of the ten directions have transcended, the wondrous and adorned road. Now listen attentively." Ānanda bowed his head to the ground and humbly received the compassionate instruction.

**Notes**

1. *Śūraṅgama* (首楞嚴): traditionally glossed as "ultimately firm and indestructible" (究竟堅固)—a samādhi that cannot be broken by anything. The commentary calls it "the king of samādhis."
2. *Placement of this passage*: in the received text (Taishō no. 945) this passage stands in the middle of the lecture-hall dialogue in Section 15, after "…so from inside the hall I can see into the distance." Following Dharma Master Yuanying (圓瑛), the text used here moves it forward as the Buddha's first response to Ānanda's request.

## 11　双征能见能爱·问出家因缘

**原文**

> 佛告阿难：汝我同气，情均天伦，当初发心，于我法中，见何胜相，顿舍世间深重恩爱？阿难白佛：我见如来三十二相，胜妙殊绝，形体映彻，犹如琉璃。常自思惟：此相非是欲爱所生。何以故？欲气粗浊，腥臊交遘，脓血杂乱，不能发生胜净妙明，紫金光聚。是以渴仰，从佛剃落。

**解析**

- **汝我同气，情均天伦：** 阿难与佛是堂兄弟，情分等同亲兄弟。
- **形体映彻，犹如琉璃：** 佛身光明透彻，如琉璃般内外映现。
- **欲气粗浊……脓血杂乱：** 以爱欲为因、由父精母血所成的身体，粗浊不净，不可能生出这样的身相。
- **紫金光聚：** 佛身紫金色光明凝聚如山。
- **是以渴仰，从佛剃落：** 《浅释》点出，这种渴仰仍是爱心，是"舍爱从爱"，也就是舍掉一种爱，又执起另一种爱。这是后文佛追问"谁为爱乐"的伏笔。

**Translation**

> The Buddha said to Ānanda, "You and I share the same blood, and our bond is that of brothers. When you first resolved to leave home and enter my Dharma, what did you see that was so excellent that you suddenly cast aside the deep and heavy bonds of worldly love?"
>
> Ānanda said to the Buddha, "I saw the Tathāgata's thirty-two marks, supremely wondrous and beyond compare, his body radiant and translucent like crystal. I would often think to myself: these marks could not have been born of desire and craving. Why? Desire is coarse and turbid—a fetid, rank coupling, a mingling of pus and blood. It could never give rise to such a pure, wondrous, and bright mass of purple-golden light. That is why I looked up to the Buddha with longing and shaved my head to follow him."

**Notes**

1. *Share the same blood* (同氣): Ānanda was the Buddha's paternal cousin.
2. *Looked up … with longing* (渴仰): the commentary observes that this is still a form of love—"abandoning one love only to take up another." Ānanda left home out of attachment to the Buddha's appearance, and the Buddha's questioning begins precisely here.

## 12　示真心为本，令以直心酬答

**原文**

> 佛言：善哉阿难！汝等当知，一切众生，从无始来，生死相续，皆由不知常住真心性净明体，用诸妄想，此想不真，故有轮转。汝今欲研无上菩提，真发明性，应当直心，酬我所问。十方如来，同一道故，出离生死，皆以直心；心言直故，如是乃至终始地位，中间永无诸委曲相。

**解析**

- 这一段是全经的总纲。
- **常住真心性净明体：** 常住而不生不灭的真心，其自性清净、光明遍照之体。《浅释》以"衣里明珠"作比：本来具足，只是不知道，所以不能受用。
- **用诸妄想，此想不真，故有轮转：** 众生所用的只是生灭的识心，所以在生死中流转。
- **真发明性：** 真正发明（开显）本性，也就是要明白菩提是怎样成就的。
- **直心：** 不加思量、不绕弯子的心。《浅释》引《维摩诘经》"直心是道场"。
- **终始地位：** 从干慧地（始）到妙觉（终）的全部修证位次。
- **中间永无诸委曲相：** 整个修证历程中，始终没有半点迂曲。

**Translation**

> The Buddha said, "Good indeed, Ānanda! All of you should know that from beginningless time all living beings have continued in birth and death, life after life, because they do not know the everlasting true mind—the pure, bright substance of their own nature—and instead use deluded thoughts. These thoughts are not real, and so there is the turning of the wheel. Now you wish to investigate unsurpassed bodhi and truly bring this nature to light. You should answer my questions with a straightforward mind. The Tathāgatas of the ten directions all share the same path, and it was with a straightforward mind that every one of them escaped birth and death. Because their minds and their words were straightforward, from the very first stage to the very last, and all along the way, there was never the slightest crookedness."

**Notes**

1. *The everlasting true mind—the pure, bright substance of their own nature* (常住真心性淨明體): this phrase is the thesis of the entire sutra. *Everlasting* (常住) means neither arising nor ceasing; the *substance* (體) is the mind's own nature, intrinsically pure and illumining. The commentary compares it to the jewel sewn into the drunkard's robe in the *Lotus Sūtra*: always present, but of no use to one who does not know it is there.
2. *Deluded thoughts* (妄想): the arising-and-ceasing discriminating consciousness that beings mistake for their mind. The first fascicles of the sutra are devoted to exposing it.
3. *From the very first stage to the very last* (終始地位): from the Stage of Dry Wisdom (乾慧地) to Wondrous Enlightenment (妙覺), the full sequence of stages set out in Fascicle Eight.

## 13　征问心目所在

**原文**

> 阿难！我今问汝，当汝发心，缘于如来三十二相，将何所见，谁为爱乐？阿难白佛言：世尊！如是爱乐，用我心目。由目观见如来胜相，心生爱乐。故我发心，愿舍生死。佛告阿难：如汝所说，真所爱乐，因于心目。若不识知心目所在，则不能得降伏尘劳。譬如国王为贼所侵，发兵讨除，是兵要当知贼所在。使汝流转，心目为咎。吾今问汝，唯心与目，今何所在？

**解析**

- **将何所见，谁为爱乐：** 佛双征"能见"与"能爱"：用什么看见的？是谁在爱乐？"乐"读去声，意为好乐。
- **用我心目：** 阿难的回答按世俗看法是对的，但后文会证明这是错误的。
- **尘劳：** "尘"有染污义，"劳"有扰乱义，即染污扰乱心性的烦恼。降伏尘劳就是了生死。
- **国王讨贼之喻：** 要讨贼，必须先知道贼在哪里。
- **心目为咎：** 让你流转生死的祸首，正是心与目。

**Translation**

> "Ānanda, I now ask you: when you made your resolve because of the Tathāgata's thirty-two marks, with what did you see them, and who was it that delighted in them?"
>
> Ānanda said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, it was with my mind and my eyes that I delighted in them. My eyes saw the Tathāgata's excellent marks, and my mind gave rise to delight. That is why I made my resolve and wished to leave birth and death behind."
>
> The Buddha said to Ānanda, "As you say, your delight truly came about through your mind and your eyes. If you do not know where your mind and eyes are, you will never be able to subdue the defilements that wear you down. It is like a king whose country is invaded by bandits: when he sends out troops to drive them off, the troops must know where the bandits are. It is your mind and your eyes that are at fault for your wandering through birth and death. So I now ask you: your mind and your eyes—where are they now?"

**Notes**

1. *With what did you see … who was it that delighted* (將何所見，誰為愛樂): the Buddha presses two questions at once, about the faculty of seeing and about the one who loves. Ānanda's commonsense answer, "my mind and my eyes," is exactly what the next several sections will dismantle.
2. *Defilements that wear you down* (塵勞): 塵 "dust," that which taints; 勞 "toil," that which agitates. Together they name the afflictions that soil and disturb the mind.

## 14　阿难执心在身内

**原文**

> 阿难白佛言：世尊！一切世间，十种异生，同将识心，居在身内；纵观如来青莲华眼，亦在佛面。我今观此浮根四尘，只在我面；如是识心，实居身内。

**解析**

- **十种异生：** 《浅释》释为十二类生中的十类：胎、卵、湿、化、有色、无色、有想、无想、非有想、非无想。阿难举十类众生，是说"人人都这样认为，不只我阿难一个人"。《浅释》认为这里暗含一点辩解的意味。
- **识心：** 生灭的分别心。
- **青莲华眼：** 佛眼修广而色青，如青莲华。
- **浮根四尘：** 《浅释》释为面上显露的眼、耳、鼻、舌四根。另一种常见读法（如圆瑛《讲义》）是：浮尘根由色、香、味、触四尘所成。英译依《浅释》，注释并列两说。这是两家注疏读法明显不同的一处，请你特别审核。

**Translation**

> Ānanda said to the Buddha, "World-Honored One, all ten kinds of beings in the world alike hold that the conscious mind dwells within the body. And when I look at the Tathāgata's blue-lotus eyes, they too are on the Buddha's face. Now, as I observe these four outward sense organs of mine, they are right here on my face. So this conscious mind must truly dwell within my body."

**Notes**

1. *Ten kinds of beings* (十種異生): per the commentary, ten of the twelve classes of beings—those born from wombs, eggs, moisture, and transformation; those with form and those without form; those with thought and those without thought; and those not with thought and those not without thought. By appealing to "all beings," Ānanda implies that his view is simply common knowledge.
2. *Four outward sense organs* (浮根四塵): the commentary takes this as the four gross sense organs visible on the face—eyes, ears, nose, and tongue. Other commentators (e.g., Yuanying) read it as "the gross sense organ, composed of the four sense-objects" (form, smell, taste, and touch), referring to the physical organ as distinct from the subtle faculty.
3. *Blue-lotus eyes* (青蓮華眼): one of the Buddha's marks—eyes long, wide, and deep blue like the petals of the blue lotus.

## 15　如来举例辩定

**原文**

> 佛告阿难：汝今现坐如来讲堂，观祇陀林，今何所在？世尊！此大重阁清净讲堂，在给孤园，今祇陀林，实在堂外。阿难！汝今堂中，先何所见？世尊！我在堂中，先见如来，次观大众；如是外望，方瞩林园。阿难！汝瞩林园，因何有见？世尊！此大讲堂，户牖开豁，故我在堂，得远瞻见。佛告阿难：如汝所言，身在讲堂，户牖开豁，远瞩林园。亦有众生，在此堂中，不见如来，见堂外者？阿难答言：世尊！在堂不见如来，能见林泉，无有是处！

**解析**

- 佛先不直接驳斥，而是用讲堂作比，让阿难自己确认一条原则：在里面的，应当先见里面，后见外面。
- **重阁：** 楼上加楼。
- **给孤园、祇陀林：** 园是给孤独长者所买，林是祇陀太子所施，所以合称"祇树给孤独园"。
- **户牖开豁：** 门窗敞开。
- **无有是处：** 绝无此理。下一段佛就用阿难自己说的这句话来破他。

**Translation**

> The Buddha said to Ānanda, "You are now sitting in the Tathāgata's lecture hall. Where is the Jeta Grove at this moment?"
>
> "World-Honored One, this great, multi-storied, pure lecture hall is in the Garden of the Benefactor of the Solitary, and the Jeta Grove is indeed outside the hall."
>
> "Ānanda, here in the hall, what do you see first?"
>
> "World-Honored One, here in the hall I first see the Tathāgata, next I see the great assembly, and only then, looking outward, do I see the grove and garden."
>
> "Ānanda, how is it that you are able to see the grove and garden?"
>
> "World-Honored One, the doors and windows of this great lecture hall are wide open, so from inside the hall I can see into the distance."
>
> The Buddha said to Ānanda, "It is just as you say: your body is in the lecture hall, the doors and windows are open, and you see the grove in the distance. But could there be a being inside this hall who does not see the Tathāgata, yet sees what is outside?"
>
> Ānanda replied, "World-Honored One, to be in the hall and not see the Tathāgata, yet see the woods and springs outside—that is impossible!"

**Notes**

1. *Jeta Grove* (祇陀林) and *Garden of the Benefactor of the Solitary* (給孤園): the grove was given by Prince Jeta and the land purchased by the elder Anāthapiṇḍada, "Benefactor of the Solitary"; hence the monastery's full name, Jetavana Anāthapiṇḍadārāma.
2. The Buddha first draws from Ānanda his own premise—whoever is inside sees what is inside first—and then, in the next section, turns that premise against him. Ānanda's words "that is impossible" (無有是處) become the Buddha's verdict.

## 16　即例破执·心不在身内

**原文**

> 阿难！汝亦如是！汝之心灵，一切明了。若汝现前所明了心，实在身内，尔时先合了知内身。颇有众生，先见身中，后观外物？纵不能见心肝脾胃，爪生发长，筋转脉摇，诚合明了，如何不知？必不内知，云何知外？是故应知，汝言觉了能知之心，住在身内，无有是处。

**解析**

- **汝之心灵，一切明了：** 《浅释》说，这里的"心灵"仍是识心，不是真心。
- **尔时先合了知内身：** 既然心在身内，就应当像人在堂中先见如来那样，先了知身内。
- **颇有众生：** 可有这样的众生吗？
- **纵不能见心肝脾胃……诚合明了：** 即使看不见内脏，指甲在长、头发在长、筋在转、脉在跳，这些总该清楚知道吧。"长"读上声，意为生长。
- **必不内知，云何知外：** 连身内都不知，怎么能知身外？
- **觉了能知之心：** 能觉、能了、能知的心，指识心。这个短语在七处征心中反复出现，英文要统一译法。

**Translation**

> "Ānanda, it is just the same with you. Your mind is perceptive and clear about everything. If this clear, knowing mind of yours were really inside your body, then it ought first to know the inside of your body. Is there any being who first sees the inside of his body and only afterward sees things outside? Even if you cannot see your heart, liver, spleen, and stomach, surely you should clearly know your nails growing, your hair lengthening, your sinews shifting, your pulse throbbing. How is it that you do not know them? If you certainly do not know what is inside, how can you know what is outside? You should therefore know that your claim—that the mind which is aware, discerning, and knowing dwells inside your body—is impossible."

**Notes**

1. *Your mind is perceptive and clear* (汝之心靈，一切明了): the commentary stresses that this "mind" is still the discriminating consciousness, not the true mind; the Buddha is examining Ānanda's mind on Ānanda's own terms.
2. *The mind which is aware, discerning, and knowing* (覺了能知之心): this phrase recurs as the refrain of all seven refutations and is rendered identically each time.
3. This is the first of the "seven refutations of the mind's location" (七處徵心). The other six—outside the body, hidden in the sense organ, inside when the eyes are closed and outside when open, wherever things come together, in the middle, and nowhere at all—follow in the same pattern.
