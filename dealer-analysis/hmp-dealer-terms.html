How consignment works

  • Ownership — consigned units remain the property of HMP Bikes until retail sale; no floorplan, no invoice on delivery.
  • Commission — dealer retains 10% of MSRP on each retail sale and remits the balance to HMP Bikes.
  • Pricing — units retail at HMP-set MSRP (table below); promotions only with prior written approval.
  • Paperwork — MCO and full registration pack ship with every unit, same as stocked units.
  • Warranty — 1 year / 10,000 mi core warranty backed by HMP Bikes, identical to stocked units.
  • Prep — Lightning MK.II arrives 100% assembled; FLASH needs handlebar + front panel only (minutes, not hours).
  • Display & demo care — dealer provides indoor floor space and keeps units in showroom condition; removable batteries charge on a standard 110V outlet.
  • Settlement window, freight-in, insurance while on floor, and recall/return notice — defined in the signed consignment agreement; discussed in person.
